The Digital Wellbeing Project

THE VANISHING CULTURE OF NEWSPAPER STANDS IN SHANGHAI

This project focuses on lost and forgotten cultures, which in many ways reflect the elderly generation who built our society yet are now overlooked.

I. Background

Many people remember the 报刊亭 (newsstands) that once lined the city’s streets, but today almost all of them have disappeared. They are not alone. CD players, cassette tapes, and other everyday objects that once shaped the daily routines of older adults have also faded with time. In a technology-dominated society, many older adults struggle to keep pace, and the world around them can feel increasingly unfamiliar.

II. Our Goal

Our goal is to bring back their memories and give them the opportunity to immerse themselves once more in the world they knew, a place of familiarity and comfort. By doing so, we hope to help the elderly generation feel seen and valued.

Ultimately, this project aims to reduce the social isolation they experience from being disconnected from new media.
