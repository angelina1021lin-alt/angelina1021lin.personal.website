I. Background

I was born in Taiwan and grew up in Shanghai. Moving between places, cultures, and fields of study has shaped how I see the world and made me curious about how people think, act, and relate to the spaces around them. I’m especially interested in where cognitive psychology, computer science, and decision-making meet, and in how a deeper understanding of people can guide the design of thoughtful, accessible solutions that improve society.

II. Research & Community

My experience spans research and community work. On a consumer behavior research project, our team designed and analyzed a survey of more than 200 respondents to understand how people think and make purchase decisions. In Shanghai, I led a student team tackling digital exclusion among older adults; together, we designed a portable newsstand that bridged familiar forms of media and an increasingly digital world. Both projects showed me the value of approaching problems from multiple angles and working alongside others to turn ideas into real-world impact.

III. Beyond the Classroom

Outside the classroom, photography and visual storytelling keep me inspired. Whether it’s shooting landscapes on film or learning to code, the process of observing, making, and finding connections between seemingly unrelated things is what draws me in. I hope to continue exploring the intersection of human behavior, technology, and creativity, and I’m always open to opportunities where I can learn, contribute, and grow alongside a thoughtful team.
